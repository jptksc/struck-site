Quick capture

Say when

Custom areas

A card each for Home, Work, the boat — sub-lists inside. Type @Work on a new line and it files itself.

Custom colors

Four levers mix the paper and its highlighter; the ink is solved to stay legible. Any card can wear its own.

Task notes

Anchored repeats

No nagging

GitHub issues
